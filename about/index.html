About QuickFixx24

A service company from Chittagong that wants repairs, rides and home care to be easy to book and safe to trust.

Where we started

QuickFixx24 began in Chittagong, near CU 1no Gate, as a booking line for cars and CNGs. Customers kept asking for help with electrical work, plumbing, AC and tech problems too, so the services grew.

Why the ID check exists

Letting a stranger into your home takes trust. Every staff member has a QuickFixx ID, and you can verify it online before they come in.

Where we are going

We now take bookings for all eight divisions and are building a partner network of drivers, technicians and caregivers in each one.
