Frequently asked questions

The things people ask us most. Cannot find yours? Call +880 1805-455811.

How do I book a service?

Fill in our Google booking form, or call or WhatsApp +880 1805-455811. We reply within 30 minutes, day or night.

How much does it cost?

Vehicle fares are fixed: CNG ৳200–300 and car ৳500–600 depending on distance. For home repair, tech and other services we give a quote after hearing the job, and you agree it before we start. There are no hidden charges.

Which areas do you cover in Bangladesh?

We started in Chittagong and take bookings for all eight divisions. Some on-site services depend on a partner near you, so we confirm your district when you book. Online services work anywhere.

Are you open at night and on holidays?

Yes. We answer bookings 24 hours a day, seven days a week, including public holidays and Eid.

How can I pay?

Cash, bKash or Nagad. You get a confirmation for each payment.

How do I know the person at my door is from QuickFixx24?

Every staff member carries a QuickFixx ID. Type the number on our verify staff page and check that the name, role and status match.

Can I cancel a booking?

Yes. Cancel at least 1 hour before the service and it is free. Later than that, a small fee may apply. Refunds take 3–5 business days.

Is home healthcare an emergency service?

No. In an emergency call 999 or go to the nearest hospital. Our home care is for planned nursing, checkups and recovery at home.

Is your legal service the same as hiring a lawyer?

No. It is information and guidance. For court work you need a licensed advocate, and we can point you to one.

How do I become a partner?

Go to the partners page and fill in the application. Approved partners get a QuickFixx staff ID.
